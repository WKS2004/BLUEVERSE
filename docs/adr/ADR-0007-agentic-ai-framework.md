# ADR-0007: Agentic AI Framework

**Status:** Proposed

## Context

The assignment permits multiple frameworks or custom orchestration. Choosing before the workflow is designed risks forcing the domain problem into a framework.

## Decision

Select a suitable and justified Agentic AI framework/orchestration approach after the business components and complete assessed workflow are defined.

## Consequences

The eventual decision must cover planning, delegation, tools, state, validation, approval, failure recovery and evaluation.
