# BLUEVERSE Roadmap

## v0 — Foundation

Authentication, authorization, permissions, API/Auth, PostgreSQL, React/Flutter foundations, Docker, gateway, CI and documentation.

## v1 — Coastal Tourism & Operations

Tourism and coastal-management workflows.

## v2 — Environmental Resilience

Environmental authority, incident, pollution and approval workflows.

## v3 — Fisheries & Coastal Livelihoods

Fisheries and coastal-resource workflows.

Marine Biodiversity Intelligence is planned as a genuine intelligence capability and is expected to be integrated during the appropriate later phase.
